---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Add a Pine Script indicator to a Darvas chart, save it and change one of its inputs."
---

In this quickstart you add a moving average crossover indicator to a chart, save it and change one of its inputs.

## 1. Open the indicator editor

- **Desktop:** in `/terminal`, choose **Indicators** in the rail on the right of the chart. If your account has Darvas V3, open the V3 panel and switch it to **Custom indicator**.
- **Mobile:** open the indicator menu, choose the **Custom** category and tap **New custom indicator**.

The editor opens with two tabs, **Darvas** and **Code**.

## 2. Paste the script

Open the **Code** tab, replace the template with this script, and choose **Add to chart**.

```pine
//@version=6
indicator("EMA cross", overlay=true)
fastLen = input.int(9, "Fast length", minval=1)
slowLen = input.int(21, "Slow length", minval=1)
fast = ta.ema(close, fastLen)
slow = ta.ema(close, slowLen)
plot(fast, "Fast", color=color.teal)
plot(slow, "Slow", color=color.orange)
plotshape(ta.crossover(fast, slow), "Buy", shape.triangleup, location.belowbar, color.green)
plotshape(ta.crossunder(fast, slow), "Sell", shape.triangledown, location.abovebar, color.red)
```

The chart shows two moving averages on the price, a green triangle under each bar where the fast one crosses above the slow one, and a red triangle above each bar where it crosses below. The button now reads **Update chart**.

If the script has an error, the editor marks the line and shows the message. Messages from `log.info()` and runner warnings are in **Logs** under the editor.

## 3. Save the indicator

Choose **Save** and give the indicator a name. An indicator you do not save is removed when you close Darvas.

A saved indicator is in the **Custom** category of the indicator menu, and you can add it to any chart and timeframe. Each change to its code is a new version that you can go back to.

## 4. Change an input

Open the menu in the editor header and choose **Inputs**, or use the settings button of the indicator in the chart legend. Set **Fast length** to 5 and save. The fast average and the triangles move.
