## Overview

Send a question; the answer is the filings that answer it, best first, each with its passages and a citation: the accession number, the day, the item or exhibit, and a link that opens the document with the passage highlighted.

The search reads the items of 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K filings and the press releases of 8-Ks. It matches the question's words, not their meaning: a filing that names more of them, more often, ranks first, and common words such as `what` or `the` are left out. Name the companies in `filters.identifiers` to search their filings of any year; without a company, the last two years are searched.

Darvas reads every new 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K as it is filed, and older ones on request. A company in the filters with no filing read yet has its newest 10-K and 10-Q read at once; `warnings` says so, and the same search a minute later finds them.

```python
import os
import requests

headers = {"X-API-KEY": os.environ["DARVAS_API_KEY"]}
body = requests.post(
    "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/search",
    headers=headers,
    json={
        "query": "What supply chain risks does Apple face?",
        "filters": {"identifiers": {"ticker": ["AAPL"]}, "document_types": ["10-K"]},
        "limit": 3,
    },
).json()
for result in body["results"]:
    print(result["title"])
    for excerpt in result["excerpts"]:
        print("  ", excerpt["citation"]["section"], "|", excerpt["text"][:160])
print(body.get("warnings"))
```
