hideApiMarker: true
---

Pass `market`: `US` (the default), `UK` or `FR`. US companies come with their CIK; UK and French ones with the ISIN of their main share. A company is `active` while its newest annual report ended in the last two years.

```python
import os
import requests

headers = {"X-API-KEY": os.environ["DARVAS_API_KEY"]}
url = "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/financials/income-statements/tickers"

for company in requests.get(url, headers=headers, params={"market": "UK"}).json()["tickers"][:5]:
    print(company["ticker"], company["name"], company["identifier"])
```
