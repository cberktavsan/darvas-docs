## Overview

Every field the [screener](/data-api/financials/search/screener) accepts, with its type and source: `income_statements`, `balance_sheets` or `cash_flow_statements` for statement fields, `financial_metrics` for ratios, and `company` for `sector`, `industry` and `ticker`.
