## Overview

Standardized cash flow statements. Outflows are negative, as on the statement: `capital_expenditure`, repurchases in `issuance_or_purchase_of_equity_shares` and `dividends_and_other_cash_distributions`. `free_cash_flow` is operating cash flow plus capital expenditure, and null when either is unknown. Most 10-Qs report cash flows only year to date; their quarters are the difference of two filings. Coverage, restatements and the checks are as for [income statements](/data-api/financials/income-statements#how-the-statements-are-built).

```python
import os
import requests

headers = {"X-API-KEY": os.environ["DARVAS_API_KEY"]}
flows = requests.get(
    "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/financials/cash-flow-statements",
    headers=headers,
    params={"ticker": "MSFT", "period": "ttm", "limit": 8},
).json()
for row in flows["cash_flow_statements"]:
    print(row["report_period"], row["net_cash_flow_from_operations"], row["capital_expenditure"], row["free_cash_flow"])
```
