## Overview

A segment value is a fact the filing reports on the product or service axis (`product`), the geographical axis (`geography`) or the business segment axis (`segment`) alone, for its own period: revenue, operating income and depreciation on the income statement; assets, goodwill and long-lived assets on the balance sheet; capital expenditure on the cash flow statement. Labels are the filing's own, such as `iPhone`, `Other countries` or `Americas`. A line the filing does not break down is null. `annual` reads 10-K, 20-F and 40-F filings, `quarterly` 10-Q filings.

```python
import os
import requests

headers = {"X-API-KEY": os.environ["DARVAS_API_KEY"]}
body = requests.get(
    "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/financials/cash-flow-statements/segments",
    headers=headers,
    params={"ticker": "AAPL", "period": "annual", "limit": 4},
).json()
for period in body["cash_flow_statement_segments"]:
    capex = period["capital_expenditure"] or {"product": [], "geography": [], "segment": []}
    print(period["fiscal_period"], [(item["label"], item["value"]) for item in capex["segment"]])
```
