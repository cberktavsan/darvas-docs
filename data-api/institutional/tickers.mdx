## Overview

The tickers [Institutional Holdings](/data-api/institutional/holdings) answers for, sorted. A security appears once its CUSIP has a US ticker; options, bonds and some funds have no ticker and are left out.

```bash
curl -H "X-API-KEY: $DARVAS_API_KEY" \
  "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/institutional-holdings/tickers"
```
