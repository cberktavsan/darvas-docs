## Overview

One row per period of a series, with its change from the prior period and from a year earlier. Pass `series`; the ids are listed by [Labor Series](/data-api/macro/labor-series).

| Group | Series | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payrolls | `payrolls_sa`, `payrolls_nsa`, `private_payrolls_sa`, `manufacturing_payrolls_sa`, `government_payrolls_sa`, `average_hourly_earnings_sa`, `average_weekly_hours_sa` | BLS establishment survey |
| Household survey | `unemployment_rate_sa`, `unemployment_rate_nsa`, `u6_rate_sa`, `participation_rate_sa`, `employment_population_ratio_sa`, `labor_force_sa`, `employed_sa`, `unemployed_sa`, `long_term_unemployed_sa` | BLS current population survey |
| JOLTS | `job_openings_sa`, `job_openings_rate_sa`, `hires_sa`, `quits_sa`, `quits_rate_sa`, `layoffs_sa` | BLS |
| Jobless claims | `initial_claims_nsa`, `continued_claims_nsa` | Department of Labor ETA 539, weekly, the sum of all states |

Monthly series are dated by the first day of the month, claims by the end of the week. `change_year` compares with 12 months or 52 weeks earlier.

## Example

```python
import os
import requests

headers = {"X-API-KEY": os.environ["DARVAS_API_KEY"]}
base = "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/macro"

payrolls = requests.get(f"{base}/labor", headers=headers, params={"series": "payrolls_sa", "start_date": "2026-01-01"}).json()
for month in payrolls["labor"]:
    print(month["date"], month["change_prior"], "thousand jobs")
```
