## Overview

One row per bank and calendar day, with the rate in effect that day. Pass `bank`; the symbols are listed by [Central Banks](/data-api/macro/interest-rate-banks).

| Coverage | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Banks | 31, from the Federal Reserve and the ECB to the Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey and Bank Indonesia |
| History | The Federal Reserve since 1954; other banks from the start of their BIS series |
| Source | BIS central bank policy rates, daily |
| Checked | Hourly |

For the Federal Reserve the rate is the midpoint of the federal funds target range since 19 December 1985, and the effective federal funds rate before. `first_published_rate` is the first rate Darvas recorded for the day.

## Example

```python
import os
import requests

headers = {"X-API-KEY": os.environ["DARVAS_API_KEY"]}
base = "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/macro"

rates = requests.get(f"{base}/interest-rates", headers=headers, params={"bank": "FED", "start_date": "2024-01-01"}).json()
changes = [r for prev, r in zip(rates["interest_rates"], rates["interest_rates"][1:]) if r["rate"] != prev["rate"]]
for change in changes:
    print(change["date"], change["rate"])
```
