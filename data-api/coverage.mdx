- **United States**: every SEC filer, active and delisted, with segments, as-reported statements, earnings and KPIs. Statements since 2009, and since 1993 for fiscal years before XBRL.
- **United Kingdom**: about 800 companies that file a tagged annual report, for years ending since 2020, with as-reported statements.
- **France**: about 290 companies that file a tagged annual report, for years ending since 2020, with as-reported statements. Amounts are in the reporting currency, EUR for most.

## Discovering available tickers

Each dataset lists the tickers it answers for under `/tickers`:

```bash
curl -H "X-API-KEY: $DARVAS_API_KEY" https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/financials/income-statements/tickers
curl -H "X-API-KEY: $DARVAS_API_KEY" "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/financials/income-statements/tickers?market=UK"
curl -H "X-API-KEY: $DARVAS_API_KEY" https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/prices/tickers
```

Statement tickers carry each company's `identifier`, a CIK for US companies and an ISIN for UK and French ones, and whether it is `active` or `inactive`.

## Share classes and successor issuers

- A US share class takes a dot or a dash, in any case. `BRK.B`, `BRK-B` and `brk.b` all name Berkshire Hathaway's B shares in company lookups, prices, dividends, splits, 13F and fund holdings, and fails to deliver.
- A successor issuer is a company that takes over another's registration and ticker with an 8-K12B or 8-K12G3. SEC files it under a new CIK and keeps the earlier filings under the old one. Darvas answers the successor with both. ExxonMobil Holdings took over `XOM` from Exxon Mobil in July 2026, so its insider trades and holdings, sale notices, 5% owners, as-reported statements and segments include Exxon Mobil's, each row with the CIK it was filed under. Its standardized statements and metrics carry Exxon Mobil's earlier years.
