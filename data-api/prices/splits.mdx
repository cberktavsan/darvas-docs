## Overview

Each split of a US stock since its first session, oldest first. From `date` on, a holder has `numerator` shares for every `denominator` held: Apple's split of 31 August 2020 is 4 for 1. A reverse split has the smaller numerator, such as 1 for 10.

[Prices](/data-api/prices/historical) are already adjusted for every split, and [dividends](/data-api/prices/dividends) carry both the amount paid and the amount per today's share. A new split appears after its first session closes; a stock that has never split answers an empty list.

## Example

```python
import os
import requests

headers = {"X-API-KEY": os.environ["DARVAS_API_KEY"]}
splits = requests.get(
    "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/splits",
    headers=headers,
    params={"ticker": "AAPL"},
).json()["splits"]
shares = 1.0
for split in splits:
    shares *= split["numerator"] / split["denominator"]
print(shares)  # what one share of the first session became
```
