## Overview

Every cash dividend a US stock has paid since its first session, oldest first. `amount` is what one share received on the `ex_date`, in `currency`. `adjusted_amount` divides it by every split since then, so it compares with today's share and with [split-adjusted prices](/data-api/prices/historical): Apple's $0.82 of August 2020 is $0.205 after the 4-for-1 split that followed.

A new dividend appears after its ex-dividend session closes. A stock that has never paid one answers an empty list. Amounts from before a split come back from split-scaled values, so the oldest can differ from the declared figure in their last digit.

## Example

```python
import os
import requests

headers = {"X-API-KEY": os.environ["DARVAS_API_KEY"]}
dividends = requests.get(
    "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/dividends",
    headers=headers,
    params={"ticker": "AAPL", "start_date": "2025-01-01"},
).json()["dividends"]
print(sum(row["amount"] for row in dividends))
```
