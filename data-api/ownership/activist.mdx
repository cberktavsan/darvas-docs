## Overview

The 13D rows of [Beneficial Ownership](/data-api/ownership/beneficial), under the key `activist_owners`. A 13D says what the owner intends in `purpose_of_transaction`: board seats, a sale of the company, or plain investment. Amendments follow every material change, so `history=true` shows how a campaign moved.

```bash
curl -H "X-API-KEY: $DARVAS_API_KEY" \
  "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/activist-ownership?ticker=VANI&history=true"
```
