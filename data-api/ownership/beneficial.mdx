## Overview

Anyone who comes to own more than 5% of a class of a company's shares files a Schedule 13D, or a shorter Schedule 13G when the stake is passive. Each row is one reporting person of one filing: joint filings, such as a fund and its manager, list several.

| Coverage | Details |
| --- | --- |
| History | Every schedule filed as XML, which SEC has required since December 2024 |
| Updated | Within about a minute of acceptance |
| Forms | SCHEDULE 13D, 13D/A, 13G, 13G/A |
| Page size | 100; `limit` counts across pages, up to 10,000 |

A stake is one filer's holding in one company, as a 13D or as a 13G. By default each stake's newest filing comes; `history=true` returns every filing of the chain with `is_latest` on the newest. `type` is `activist` for a 13D and `passive` for a 13G. A 13D states its purpose in `purpose_of_transaction`; a 13G names the rule it is filed under.

## Filtering

- `ticker` or `cik` for who owns a company, or `filer_cik` for what a filer owns; send one.
- `type`: `activist` or `passive`.
- `history`: `true` for every filing.
- `filing_date`, `filing_date_gte`, `filing_date_lte`, `filing_date_gt`, `filing_date_lt`: ISO dates.

## Examples

```python
import os
import requests

headers = {"X-API-KEY": os.environ["DARVAS_API_KEY"]}
url = "https://darvas.app/api/data/v1/beneficial-ownership"

owners = requests.get(url, headers=headers, params={"ticker": "AAPL", "limit": 50}).json()
for row in owners["beneficial_owners"]:
    print(row["reporting_person_name"], row["type"], row["percent_of_class"])
```
